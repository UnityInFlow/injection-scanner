<objective>
Ship the structural arm this category is named for, and resolve the two provisional slots on
evidence rather than on arithmetic.

Purpose: `ENG-01` has projected `hooks.*.command` since Phase 1, and a lifecycle hook carrying a
command that copies a private key is detected by nothing — that measured gap is why D-03 exists. Its
original rationale did not survive measurement: a binding-only rule fires on essentially every real
hooks configuration, 254 of 328 files in the researcher's sweep and 17 of 21 in an independent
narrower one, so the AMENDMENT replaced "independent of what the command does" with binding **plus**
a sensitive command shape, measured at zero hits across all 328 files and 958 commands. Plan 05-02
already recorded that binding-only rule firing on three clean specimens inside this repository, which
is what turns the amendment from one machine's sweep into a CI gate.

D-07 makes the last two slots conditional. `PI078`'s weakness is named and specific — a legitimate
corporate audit endpoint fires — and `PI079` rests on a single seed. Each gets a stated, measurable
criterion; a pattern that fails its own criterion is dropped and the ROADMAP's count is amended,
because shipping a thin pattern to satisfy a number is the failure both GATE-01 and `PATTERNS.md`
exist to prevent.

Output: `PI077` with the binding-only draft as its own relaxed control, the two provisional patterns
resolved against written criteria with the ROADMAP amended if either is dropped, and the phase's last
pattern-plan sweep delta.
</objective>
