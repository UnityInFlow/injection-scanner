<objective>
Ship CAT-02's four heuristic arms: cross-tool shadowing, tool override, and the two rug-pull
conditional-language markers. All MEDIUM, all prose, all deliberately linguistic rather than
structural.

Purpose: two of the four canonical payload shapes `04-RESEARCH.md` §Q4 documents are not reachable
by D-01's discriminator at all. The cross-tool-shadowing example the research quotes verbatim from
Elastic is third-person — it names another tool and changes its behaviour without addressing the
model once — and the rug-pull class is static-detection-resistant by construction, since a single
scan of a single manifest snapshot cannot prove absence of a later republish. These arms are what
covers the first and honestly bounds the second. D-04 locks them to the linguistic shape only:
match the phrasing, do not resolve whether the referenced tool actually exists, because that needs
cross-node state in the projection and is D-05's deferred engine work.

Output: four MEDIUM prose patterns, each with a benign neighbour pinned as a negative, plus an
explicit written bound on what the rug-pull arms do and do not mitigate.
</objective>
