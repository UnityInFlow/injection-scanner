## Two constraints that will bite

1. **`matched_text` must carry ORIGINAL bytes, not decoded ones.** `--baseline` digests
   `matched_text`; if it held the decoded form, every baselined finding would become a free pass
   for its whole obfuscation family. `scanner.rs` already does this for the normalized pass via
   `original_slice` — follow that.
2. **Integration shape from ENG-01:** add a gated pass in `Scanner::scan_with_confidence`. The
   structural pass added a
   `if !self.compiled.iter().any(|cp| cp.scope == PatternScope::Frontmatter)` guard so it costs
   nothing when unused. Do the same, or every scan pays for decoding it never needs.
