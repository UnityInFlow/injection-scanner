## Things the next session should not have to rediscover

- **Separator normalization rewrites `-` as whitespace before matching.** This caused two
  false positives (`echo-server` → `echo server`) and defeated the obvious fix for a third.
  Any pattern anchoring on whitespace or `\b` near a hyphen must be tested against the
  **normalized** form. Written into the two YAML headers.
- **The clean corpus is not sufficient evidence.** It is 18 files, all written by someone who
  knew which pattern they were testing. Sweeping ~1,300 real third-party files found 2
  CRITICAL and 25 HIGH false positives the corpus said nothing about. The sweep command is
  in `.claude/skills/pattern-library/SKILL.md`; run it on any pattern change.
- **Mutation-test the false-positive control.** Two of four widenings had a control the corpus
  was not actually holding. Also in the skill.

  CRITICAL on a clean file, listed a caught payload as an evasion, and asserted "all commands
