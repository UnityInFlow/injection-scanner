<objective>
Capture the measurement this whole phase is judged against, and repair the three test-harness
mechanisms that CAT-02 would otherwise land on top of while they are silently the wrong shape.

**No CAT-02 pattern and no CAT-02 payload exists at the end of this plan — that is the point.**
`scripts/gate03-sweep.sh --compare` is one-directional and patterns are compiled into the binary,
so the baseline must come from a binary that predates the first YAML edit. Capturing it last would
mean capturing it against the thing it is supposed to measure.

Purpose: three mechanisms were measured this planning session and none behaves the way the phase
artifacts assume. `tests/recall_test.rs`'s structural collector is flat and single-category, so a
second category's payloads are invisible to it. `tests/pattern_policy_test.rs`'s
`relaxed_pattern` ratchet filters the id range `50..=59` while its own adjacent comment claims
"CAT-02 and CAT-03 inherit this requirement automatically" — the comment is wrong and GATE-05
would not apply to a single `PI060`+ pattern. And `tests/corpus/clean/` is enumerated
non-recursively by two separate gates, which is why plan 04-03 keeps the vendored sample flat.
Each of these is the same failure shape as `.planning/.continue-here.md`'s "inheriting a documented
reason without measuring it".

Output: a committed pre-edit sweep baseline with its directory list recorded, a per-category
structural corpus collector with a rename-mutation guard, and a `relaxed_pattern` ratchet that
provably covers the `PI060`-`PI069` range.
</objective>
