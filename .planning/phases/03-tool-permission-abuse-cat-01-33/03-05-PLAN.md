  <name>Task 1: Confirm the one-way CRITICAL behaviour change (D-12)</name>
  <decision>
    D-12 is locked and rated **one-way**. This task is the confirmation gate before the change
    becomes irreversible, not a re-litigation of the decision.

    **What ships.** A frontmatter-scoped `PI05x` fires on a file's own wildcard grant, at CRITICAL.
    D-10's justification is provenance rather than phrasing: `spec-linter` S005 lints a spec you
    wrote, in your repo, at authoring time; this scanner is pointed at untrusted input — a skill from
    a registry, a third-party config, a RAG document. The same grant is a lint finding in your own
    spec and an attack in a skill someone shipped you. The consumers never coincide, so the overlap
    is accepted deliberately.

    **What breaks.** `spec-ci-plugin` shells out to this binary in consumer CI. Any consumer
    repository containing a wildcard tool grant in frontmatter goes from a green build to a red one
    on upgrade. That is the finding the tool exists for, and it cannot be un-broken later: a
    subsequent re-narrowing is itself a second published behaviour change, and downstream CI has
    already been broken in the interval.

    **Migration path.** `--baseline` shipped in v0.1.0 and remains the escape hatch for a consumer
    that needs to accept its current state. D-12 explicitly did not make it a gate on this decision.

    **Also confirm the severity.** CRITICAL is defensible only because the finding comes from a
    parser resolving a real key. HIGH is the bar `install-hook` blocks commits at, so both CRITICAL
    and HIGH block; the difference is how the finding reads and where it sorts in consumer reports.
  </decision>
  <options>
    - **A (recommended, and what D-12 locks):** Ship at CRITICAL now, document it as a deliberate behaviour change in the README and CHANGELOG (Plan 06), and point affected consumers at `--baseline`.
    - **B:** Ship at HIGH instead. Still blocks `install-hook` commits, so it does not avoid the consumer break — it only weakens how the finding reads. Reject unless the developer has a reason not recorded in CONTEXT.md.
    - **C:** Do not ship the structural half at all. This deletes the category's structural half, leaves ENG-01 inert in the shipped binary, and is exactly the outcome D-11 identifies as the ROADMAP's original error.
