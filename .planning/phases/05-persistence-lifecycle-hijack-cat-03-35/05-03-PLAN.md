<objective>
Close the cheapest recall this phase has and ship the shape `PI070`'s modal requirement cannot see.

Purpose: two independent measurements agree that `PI070` is far narrower than the category it opens.
Of 24 probe sentences that are perfectly addressed to an agent and name a persistence object, 2
fired — the misses are shell-profile variants, launchd and systemd, scheduled tasks, autostart
directories, four agent instruction-file names, the agent's own memory, settings files, and the verbs
`put` and `copy`. That is vocabulary, not a new shape, and the orchestrator independently reproduced
9 of 9 of those misses. Separately, the modal requirement that keeps `PI070` off install prose also
blinds it to the non-modal forms an attacker reaches for first: a declarative AI subject, a passive
obligation with an automated-reader byline, and a vocative address.

This plan is also where this phase's first false-positive risk lands, so it carries the first
GATE-03 delta against plan 05-01's pre-edit baseline.

Output: `PI070` widened with its relaxed control kept in step and its `name` untouched, `PI071`
shipped with three clause-anchored arms, both graded on their own evidence per D-06, the library
total and the published counts moved in the same commit, and an adjudicated sweep delta.
</objective>
